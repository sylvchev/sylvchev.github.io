Manon Verbockhaven successfully defended a PhD thesis on the expressivity of neural networks and how to fix it optimally by growing the architecture, co-supervised with Guillaume Charpiat. The core results are in [Growing Tiny Networks](https://hal.science/hal-04591472v2) (TMLR).
