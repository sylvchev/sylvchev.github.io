I am happy to be one of the organizers of the [EEG/EMG Foundation Challenge 2026](https://neural-interfaces26.github.io/index.html), now open! Can models trained on neurophysiological signals generalize to new people, sessions, tasks and conditions? Four tracks (EEG-to-image, BCI decoding, sleep onset, EMG-to-pose), a $20,000 prize pool, and results at the NeurIPS 2026 Brain & Body Workshop in Sydney. Organized by Yneuro, Inria and UC San Diego, with Meta and many partners; hosted on [Codabench](https://www.codabench.org/).
